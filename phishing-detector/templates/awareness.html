Understanding phishing attacks is your first line of defense. Learn how attackers operate and how to stay safe online.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of social engineering attack where cybercriminals impersonate legitimate organisations — banks, social networks, government agencies, or trusted companies — to trick victims into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data.

The term comes from "fishing" — attackers cast a wide net and wait for victims to bite. According to the FBI's Internet Crime Report, phishing is consistently one of the most prevalent and costly cybercrime categories, costing businesses and individuals billions of dollars annually.
